# Contract: BMAD Repository Setup

## Objective

Establish stable BMAD v6 with the BMad Method module as the repository-wide,
version-controlled default for planning and implementing future extensions to
`forgetest`. The setup will serve both Codex and Claude Code, preserve approved
plans and implementation stories as durable decision history, and add automated
governance without replacing the repository's existing architecture, security,
study-protocol, documentation, or CI authorities. Source: Reconnaissance
Dossier, Objective and Recommended Scope; Grilling Consensus, Idea Verdict and
Revised Solution.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

1. **FR-001 — Exact stable installation.** The repository shall use one exact,
   inspected stable v6 installer release and the BMM module only. The selected
   installer artifact identity, integrity metadata, resolved executable
   dependency graph or equivalent immutable bundle, exact Node and npm versions,
   supported platform, generation environment, baseline repository revision,
   enabled module, enabled tools, generated-path inventory, and generated-file
   hashes shall be recorded in the committed root `bmad.lock.json`. Source:
   Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 3–4
   and Revised Solution 1–2; Consigliere Assessment, Finding 2; official
   installation behavior cited by the Reconnaissance Dossier, Dependencies.
2. **FR-002 — Disposable bootstrap.** Initial package retrieval, provenance
   review, content inspection, and regeneration comparison shall occur outside
   the working repository before the accepted exact installer is allowed to
   modify the checkout. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 3–4 and
   Revised Solution 1 and 12.
3. **FR-003 — Shared tool integrations.** Codex and Claude Code shall receive
   tracked BMAD launchers generated from the same locked BMM installation. The
   repository shall verify parity by workflow identity, module version, project
   context reference, authority precedence, and study prohibition. Source:
   Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 5 and Revised Solution 2 and 8;
   Reconnaissance Dossier, Risks and Unknowns.
4. **FR-004 — Repository onboarding.** A tracked repository-level instruction
   surface for Codex, an equivalent Claude Code instruction surface, and concise
   README onboarding shall identify BMAD as the default extension workflow and
   direct agents to the project context, authoritative documents, artifact
   index, workflow tiers, verification entry point, and upgrade policy. Source:
   Reconnaissance Dossier, Recommended Scope 5; Grilling Consensus, Revised
   Solution 13.
5. **FR-005 — Brownfield project context.** A committed project context shall
   reference the six-crate workspace boundaries, architecture, security policy,
   study protocols, public-documentation boundary, and CI proof contract. It
   shall state that those repository sources override generated BMAD guidance
   and record reviewed identities for drift detection. Source: `Cargo.toml:1-19`;
   `ARCHITECTURE.md:14-170`; `SECURITY.md:22-150`;
   `studies/rust-agent-v1/README.md:43-59`;
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot/README.md:3-10` and
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot/README.md:21-56`; `docs/book.toml:5`;
   `docs/src/SUMMARY.md:1-11`; `.github/workflows/ci.yml:16-209`;
   Grilling Consensus, Revised Solution 4 and 10.
6. **FR-006 — Durable artifact governance.** Approved planning artifacts and
   implementation stories shall be committed under the BMAD output tree and
   registered in the canonical `_bmad-output/index.yaml`. Each record shall
   include a stable ID,
   artifact type, title, path, lifecycle status, owner, approver, approval time,
   approval evidence, scope, parent record when applicable, supersession links,
   and information classification. Drafts shall have no approver, approval time,
   or approval evidence. The committed, human-owned
   `_bmad-output/authorities.yaml` registry shall map stable human identities and
   identity types to Don, repository-maintainer, scoped-maintainer, and
   security-owner roles and their permitted scopes. The Don shall approve the
   initial registry and every registry change. An artifact
   approver shall be authorized by that registry for the artifact's scope and
   shall differ from the owner unless the registry's Don explicitly approves and
   records the exception as evidence. Source: Don's Phase A decision; Grilling
   Consensus, Attack Assessment 1 and 13 and Revised Solution 6; Consigliere
   Assessment, first review Finding 3 and second review Finding 2.
7. **FR-007 — Deterministic artifact lifecycle.** Supported lifecycle states
   shall distinguish drafts, approved records, superseded records, and archived
   records. An artifact shall be effective only when the canonical index marks
   it approved and no approved successor of the same artifact type and scope
   supersedes it. At most one effective record may exist for each artifact-type
   and scope pair. Parent plans, epics, and child stories may remain effective
   together because each has a distinct type and scope; child records shall name
   their parent. Supersession shall require the same approval authority as the
   affected scope. Archival shall retain the original approval evidence and
   require the same authority as supersession; an archival record shall identify
   its authorizer and authorization evidence. Superseded and archived history
   shall remain in Git, archival shall preserve index traceability, and schema
   changes shall include a migration record. Source: Grilling Consensus, Revised
   Solution 6; Consigliere Assessment, first review Finding 3 and second review
   Finding 1.
8. **FR-008 — Workflow tiers.** Full BMM planning shall be required for features,
   behavioral or architectural changes, public APIs, dependency changes,
   security-sensitive changes, and cross-crate work. A documented lightweight
   exemption may cover typo-only, formatting-only, and mechanically generated
   changes only when no mandatory category also applies. Tier precedence shall
   be: frozen-study prohibition first, urgent-security procedure second,
   mandatory full-planning categories third, and trivial exemption last. A
   trivial exemption shall record rationale and approval from a repository
   maintainer authorized by the authority registry and other than the author,
   unless the registry's Don explicitly approves it.
   Urgent security remediation may use a minimal incident record with security
   owner or Don approval from the registry. The incident record shall contain a
   concrete retrospective deadline. Five business days means the next five
   Monday-through-Friday calendar days in the Europe/London timezone, with no
   additional holiday exclusions; the recorded deadline is authoritative after
   validation against that rule. Frozen-study modification shall have no
   exemption. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 2 and Revised
   Solution 5; Consigliere Assessment, first review Finding 4 and second review
   Findings 2 and 4.
9. **FR-009 — Study immutability.** Every shared instruction surface, project
   context, and validation mechanism shall explicitly prohibit BMAD workflows
   from mutating or using either `studies/rust-agent-v1` or
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot` as scratch space. The same protection shall apply
   automatically to every future study whose protocol declares itself frozen,
   locked, or pre-registered. Any change to a protected task, grader, prompt,
   reference patch, model, effort, image, budget, retry policy, endpoint,
   publication gate, or other frozen design term shall create a new study.
   Source: `studies/rust-agent-v1/README.md:43-59`;
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot/README.md:3-10`,
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot/README.md:21-56`, and
   `studies/rust-agent-pilot/README.md:91-104`; Reconnaissance Dossier, Codebase
   Overview; Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 8 and Revised Solution 4;
   Consigliere Assessment, Finding 1.
10. **FR-010 — Dedicated BMAD verification.** The repository shall provide one
    automated verification entry point and a CI gate covering lock-to-manifest
    consistency, generated-file integrity, launcher parity, allowed tracked
    Claude files, artifact index/schema integrity, effective-artifact
    uniqueness, authority-registry integrity and eligibility, approval and
    transition evidence, content classification, sensitive-content checks,
    study prohibitions, context drift, and publication boundaries. Every indexed
    or generated path shall be a canonical repository-relative path contained
    in an explicitly approved root. Verification shall reject absolute paths,
    traversal, symlinks, missing targets, duplicate canonical targets, and any
    overlap between generated and human-owned paths. Source: Grilling Consensus,
    Attack Assessment 6–12 and Revised Solution 3 and 7–11; Consigliere
    Assessment, first review Finding 5 and second review Findings 1–2.
11. **FR-011 — Safe upgrades.** Regeneration shall occur only in a disposable
    clean clone at the pinned repository revision. Stable patch upgrades shall
    repeat provenance review, regenerate, compare, migrate artifacts when
    required, and receive human review. A major BMAD upgrade shall require a new
    approved plan. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 10 and 13 and
    Revised Solution 12.
12. **FR-012 — Internal publication boundary.** BMAD framework and output
    artifacts shall remain outside the public mdBook by default. Verification
    shall detect navigation entries, documentation links, build-script copy
    rules, or rendered output that unintentionally publishes internal BMAD
    material. Source: `docs/book.toml:5`; `docs/src/SUMMARY.md:1-11`;
    Reconnaissance Dossier, Risks and Unknowns; Grilling Consensus, Attack
    Assessment 12 and Revised Solution 11.

### Non-Functional Requirements

1. **NFR-001 — Supply-chain integrity.** No unpinned or uninspected BMAD package
   may execute against the repository. The accepted package identity and
   integrity evidence shall be reviewable from committed metadata. Source:
   Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 3–4.
2. **NFR-002 — Secret safety.** Local Claude and Codex permission, credential,
   and machine-specific state shall remain untracked. Only explicitly enumerated
   shared launcher paths may bypass existing ignore rules, and committed BMAD
   material shall be checked for secrets and prohibited data. Source:
   `.gitignore:15-20`; `SECURITY.md:22-150`; Grilling Consensus, Attack
   Assessment 6–7.
3. **NFR-003 — Information classification.** Durable BMAD artifacts may be
   classified only as public or repository-visible. Repository-visible means
   excluded from public documentation but visible to everyone with repository
   access; it is not a confidentiality or access-control boundary. Credentials,
   personal data, customer-confidential information, live exploit material, raw
   private evidence, and any material requiring restricted storage shall remain
   outside Git. Source: `SECURITY.md:22-150`; Grilling Consensus, Revised
   Solution 7; Consigliere Assessment, Finding 7.
4. **NFR-004 — Reproducibility.** A clean disposable environment matching the
   locked installer artifact, dependency identity, Node/npm runtime, supported
   platform, generation environment, and repository revision shall reproduce
   generated framework and launcher files without altering human-owned planning
   artifacts. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 10 and Revised
   Solution 12; Consigliere Assessment, Finding 2.
5. **NFR-005 — Drift visibility.** Changes to any authoritative source recorded
   by the project context shall require an explicit project-context review before
   BMAD verification passes. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 11
   and Revised Solution 10.
6. **NFR-006 — Compatibility preservation.** The BMAD setup shall not change
   product runtime behavior, crate APIs, benchmark results, existing test
   semantics, or the current mdBook publication set. Source: Reconnaissance
   Dossier, Recommended Scope 6; `ARCHITECTURE.md:14-170`;
   `.github/workflows/ci.yml:16-209`.
7. **NFR-007 — Cross-tool consistency.** Codex and Claude Code integrations
   shall expose the same intended BMM workflows and repository-specific
   governance at the same locked version. Source: Grilling Consensus, Attack
   Assessment 5 and Revised Solution 8.
8. **NFR-008 — Maintainability.** Generated framework files, human-owned
   project context, durable artifacts, transient outputs, and local tool state
   shall have documented ownership and non-overlapping lifecycle rules. Source:
   Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 10 and 13.

## Architectural Decisions

1. **AD-001 — CI-Enforced Repository Standard.** Adopt Sit-Down Approach B
   because BMAD is intended as long-term shared infrastructure and therefore
   needs automated enforcement rather than review-only governance. Source: Don's
   Sit-Down selection on 2026-08-14.
2. **AD-002 — BMM-only initial scope.** Install core BMAD plus BMM and defer BMB,
   CIS, GDS, TEA, and other optional modules until a documented need exists. The
   repository already has extensive domain-specific test and evidence controls.
   Source: Reconnaissance Dossier, Risks and Unknowns and Recommended Scope 1;
   `.github/workflows/ci.yml:16-209`.
3. **AD-003 — Generated framework tracked, human history protected.** Commit the
   shared framework and launcher surface, but distinguish regenerable files from
   human-owned project context and approved artifacts so upgrades cannot silently
   overwrite decisions. Source: Grilling Consensus, Revised Solution 2, 6, and
   12.
4. **AD-004 — Canonical index over directory inference.** Determine approval,
   effectiveness, supersession, ownership, and classification from a validated
   index rather than filenames or directory presence. Source: Grilling
   Consensus, Attack Assessment 1 and 13.
5. **AD-005 — Existing repository authority retained.** BMAD orchestrates work;
   it does not redefine architecture, security, study validity, public
   documentation, or proof gates. Source: Reconnaissance Dossier, Codebase
   Overview and Applicable Negative Constraints; Grilling Consensus, Idea
   Verdict Integration.
6. **AD-006 — Verification extends existing CI.** Add a focused BMAD governance
   gate while retaining all existing Rust and proof jobs. Source:
   `.github/workflows/ci.yml:16-209`; Grilling Consensus, Attack Assessment 9.

## Grilling Conclusions

### Key Decisions

- **Repository-wide default:** BMAD governs future extension planning and
  implementation because the Don selected it as the go-to approach.
- **Durable team history:** Shared framework files, project context, approved
  plans, and implementation stories are committed so decisions survive sessions
  and contributors.
- **Exact inspected bootstrap:** A disposable provenance review and immutable
  lock precede repository mutation because the first installation is itself a
  supply-chain event.
- **Automated governance:** CI validates lock integrity, launchers, artifacts,
  study boundaries, context drift, and publication boundaries because manual
  conventions alone do not scale reliably.
- **Existing sources remain authoritative:** BMAD coordinates future work but
  cannot override the project's established engineering and evidence contracts.

### Rejected Alternatives

- **Lightweight Governed BMAD:** Rejected because manual approval, drift, and
  parity checks are too weak for a repository-wide default intended to persist.
- **Hermetic BMAD Vendoring:** Rejected because retaining an installer source
  snapshot adds repository and upgrade burden not justified by the current
  threat model; exact version and integrity locking plus disposable inspection
  provide the selected balance.
- **Optional test-architecture module at bootstrap:** Rejected because it
  overlaps with the existing Rust test, Docker proof, calibration, audit, and
  evidence systems without a demonstrated additional requirement.
- **Local-only BMAD:** Rejected by the Don's requirement for team-wide tracked
  infrastructure and durable decision history.
- **Mutable stable or major-version installation:** Rejected because it cannot
  reproduce the first repository mutation.

### Unresolved Objections

- None. The Synthesizer reported zero unresolved CRITICAL objections after all
  thirteen Devil's-Advocate attacks were incorporated.

## Out of Scope

- Adding optional BMAD modules such as BMB, CIS, GDS, or TEA.
- Designing or implementing a future `forgetest` product extension.
- Changing Rust source, crate APIs, runtime behavior, evaluation algorithms,
  benchmark results, or report schemas.
- Editing the locked v1 study, the pre-registered pilot, or any future protected
  study; or executing the planned paid-agent studies.
- Publishing BMAD planning or implementation artifacts through the public
  mdBook.
- Vendoring the full BMAD npm package or its dependency graph in the repository.
- Changing remote GitHub branch protection, repository permissions, or secrets.
- Automating artifact approval without accountable human review.

## Open Risks

- **Upstream installer evolution — MEDIUM.** Even stable patch releases may alter
  generated paths or semantics. Mitigation: immutable locking, disposable
  inspection, isolated upgrade review, and major-version replanning.
- **Governance maintenance burden — MEDIUM.** Index, drift, and parity controls
  require upkeep. Mitigation: one verification entry point, clear ownership, and
  additive CI enforcement.
- **False positives in sensitive-content checks — LOW.** Security and benchmark
  terminology may resemble secrets or exploit content. Mitigation: documented
  reviewable exceptions that cannot include actual credentials or prohibited
  data.
- **BMAD workflow churn — MEDIUM.** Upstream workflow changes may require launcher
  or artifact migrations. Mitigation: versioned migration records and
  regeneration only from inspected exact releases.
- **Contributor friction — LOW.** The default process adds steps to extension
  work. Mitigation: explicit trivial-change and urgent-security tiers while
  preserving non-negotiable study rules.
